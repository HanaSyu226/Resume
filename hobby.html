i-CATS Library

Student Lounge Concept

By Aina Mardia Mohamad Kerta · Renders: Coohom

Sketched for my MPP (Majlis Perwakilan Pelajar) selection campaign — a reimagined lounge corner for the i-CATS Library. Months later, I watched pieces of it come to life: my concept was pitched to the Student Council and Affairs, and when Maznah Library was revamped, the meeting room, individual computer stations and lounge sofas I'd envisioned were built in. Not every detail survived — but the library also gained a student snooker area and a small café elsewhere on campus.

"A student sketch, partly built into a real space students now use."

Built In Real Life

My Room Redesign

By Aina Mardia Mohamad Kerta · Renders: Coohom

This one didn't stay a concept. Before touching a single piece of furniture, I sketched my room exactly how I wanted it — layout, placement, everything — and then rebuilt it in real life to match. I redo my room every year now, and this has become my go-to way to plan the change before committing to it.

"It came out just as I'd designed it."

Small Space, Big Character

By Aina Mardia Mohamad Kerta · Renders: Coohom

Two designs, one question: how far can small-space creativity go? The first squeezes a 1-bedroom, 1-bathroom unit with a compact kitchen into something that still feels classy, not cramped. The second leans into the loft style now booming across Sarawak, prized by students and working adults for its low rental.

"Proof that budget-friendly doesn't have to mean design-poor."

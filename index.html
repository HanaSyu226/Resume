Dear Members of the Hiring Committee,

It is with genuine enthusiasm that I submit my application for the position of Lecturer in Computing and Software Engineering. I write not as a stranger to i-CATS University College, but as one of its own: an alumna of the Bachelor of Software Engineering (Hons.) programme and, since January 2026, a part-time lecturer on its teaching staff. This position represents the natural deepening of a commitment I have already begun.

In my current capacity I deliver two modules — Introduction to Computer Systems & Applications (Foundation) and Digital Literacy (Bridging) — designing course materials, leading lectures and tutorials, and administering assessments aligned with programme outcomes. Teaching at the institution where I trained affords me an intimate understanding of the i-CATS student journey, and I am comfortable delivering both the theoretical and practical components of the curriculum.

My classroom practice is grounded in recent industry experience. As a Research & Development Intern at Sarawak Energy Berhad, I designed and delivered an executive Project Overview dashboard in Microsoft Power BI — and, while still completing that internship, I was offered a Software Developer position at Satok Digital Bridge, a role I held from May 2025 to January 2026.

In September 2026 I commence the Master of Information Technology by research — alongside a record of state-level innovation medals, media recognition, and community volunteering.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my teaching record at i-CATS, my industry grounding, and my research trajectory can contribute to the programme.
